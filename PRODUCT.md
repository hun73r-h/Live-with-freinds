# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The product is for the creator and their friends who want to watch a shared screen together and talk from separate locations.

## Product Purpose

Let a small group quickly join a browser room by link or room code, watch the host's shared screen, and communicate through voice and chat.

## Positioning

A lightweight watch-party for friends that works without accounts or a database: create a room, share an invite, and watch and talk together.

## Operating Context

People open the web app from an invite link or enter a room code. The host shares their screen; other participants can join the room, use their microphones, and chat.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No login and no database.
- Rooms are joined by invite link or room code.
- Only the host can share their screen.
- Participants can use voice chat and room text chat.
- The WebRTC mesh is intended for small friend groups, not large audiences.
- TURN relay configuration may be needed for participants on restrictive or incompatible networks.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Live with frens.
- The project includes a logo asset at `logo.svg`.

## Evidence on Hand

- The repository contains the running web app and its room, screen-share, voice, and chat implementation.
- No customer testimonials, usage metrics, or other external proof are present in the project.

## Product Principles

- Make joining a room quick and low-friction.
- Make it clear who is in the room and who is hosting.
- Keep screen sharing and conversation central to the shared watch-party.
- Keep the experience suitable for a small, trusted group of friends.
